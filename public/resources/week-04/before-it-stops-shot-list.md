# Before It Stops — complete scene shot list

Version 1.0 · 2026-09-12 · Authored teaching/production-planning draft

Source: `resources/week-03/scene.txt`. This list covers the entire current
single-scene screenplay, not an unwritten longer film. Five shots, in screen
order. Each sketch is a rough staging thumbnail, not generated footage or
proof of a tested take. This is teaching material, not OWN-W04 owner evidence.

## Scene and continuity

INT. MOTHER'S APARTMENT — KITCHEN — NIGHT. Nadia, in her 30s, finishes packing
on the final night before the apartment is cleared. Theo is an incoming
caller; he is not shown and no reply is added. Ending: Nadia accepts on the
last ring, says “Hey,” then listens, phone pressed to her ear; fade out.

Screen layout for this staging draft: Nadia sits behind the table; the last
box is on screen-left and the phone on screen-right, within her right hand's
reach. Empty shelves and boxes by the doorway are visible in the wide.
Keep this relationship and hand consistent across the inserts. These are
explicit staging choices for the draft, not newly established story facts.
Do not use the separate shot-size illustrations as continuity references:
they contain different actions and room layouts.

Select a shared character, costume, room, phone and lighting reference before
production. Those visual designs remain pending. Same phone throughout;
its position deliberately changes from table to ear. Incoming screen text:
THEO CALLING. Acceptance ends the ringtone. Do not introduce a smile or imply
that the estrangement is resolved.

## 01 — Wide / establishing shot

**Picture/action:** Empty shelves, boxes by the door, Nadia and the table in
one readable frame. Nadia presses tape across the last box, sets it on the
table and settles. The phone beside it lights up and begins ringing. Hold
long enough to register the room before cutting to its screen.

**Purpose:** Establish the imminent departure surrounding Nadia's decision.

**Camera/blocking:** Fixed, eye-level wide; show seated body, table and room.
Nadia's hands move from the box to rest; no camera move.

**Sound/cut:** Tape and box contact give way to the incoming ringtone. Cut
when the lit phone attracts attention, without adding a redundant angle.

**Rough thumbnail:**

```text
+----------------------------------+
| empty shelves       door / boxes |
|                      [] []       |
|                 O Nadia          |
|                /|\               |
|       [last box]        [phone*]  |
|  ========= TABLE =============   |
|               chair              |
+----------------------------------+
* screen lights up; camera fixed
```

## 02 — Insert / incoming-call screen

**Picture/action:** The same phone on the same table, screen-up, displaying
THEO CALLING. Do not show a different case or change its orientation.

**Purpose:** Identify the estranged brother as the source of the interruption.

**Camera/blocking:** Static close detail of the phone, screen legible, with
little surrounding table. No hand touching it yet. Choose the angle to keep
screen orientation consistent with shot 01.

**Sound/cut:** Ringtone continues across both cuts. Cut to Nadia after the
caller identity is readable.

**Rough thumbnail:**

```text
+----------------------------------+
|             tabletop             |
|           +------------+         |
|           |    THEO    |         |
|           |  CALLING   |         |
|           +------------+         |
|         no hand touching         |
+----------------------------------+
```

## 03 — Close-up / held reaction

**Picture/action:** Nadia looks toward the phone and remains still. Frame
her face clearly; preserve the eyeline toward screen-right/down. No added
smile, tears or reaction gesture.

**Purpose:** Make her refusal to respond visible after she knows who is calling.

**Camera/blocking:** Fixed face-and-upper-shoulder close-up. The body stays
still; the ongoing ring supplies pressure without another phone shot.

**Sound/cut:** Ringtone continues. Hold the reaction, then cut to the hand as
its movement begins. Do not repeat the full reach in both shots.

**Rough thumbnail:**

```text
+----------------------------------+
|             hair                 |
|           /------\               |
|          | eyes ↘ |              |
|          |  face  |              |
|           \------/               |
|          shoulders               |
+----------------------------------+
↘ eyeline to phone; camera fixed
```

## 04 — Insert / reach and stop

**Picture/action:** Nadia's right hand drifts toward the phone, then stops
short of touching it. Include wrist, palm and fingers so the movement and
gap are unmistakable. Phone stays on the table; do not pick it up here.

**Purpose:** Turn the conflict into an interrupted attempt to make contact.

**Camera/blocking:** Static table-level or shallow overhead detail, from the
same side of the action axis. Hand enters from Nadia's side of the table;
the phone remains screen-right. End on the stopped hand.

**Sound/cut:** Ringtone continues into its final ring. Its cadence provides
urgency; “one ring left” is story timing, not unexplained on-screen text.
Cut into shot 05 before the grasp, preserving the hand's stopped position.

**Rough thumbnail:**

```text
+----------------------------------+
|                                  |
| wrist--palm--fingers ---> STOP    |
|                          gap     |
|                         [phone]  |
|             tabletop             |
+----------------------------------+
---> hand movement only; camera fixed
```

## 05 — Insert to close-up / one continuous upward tilt

**Picture/action:** Start tightly on the stopped hand and phone, matching
shot 04. Nadia completes the reach, accepts the call with a clearly readable
screen tap, grasps the phone and lifts it to her right ear. The camera tilts
up continuously with the lift and settles on her face as she says “Hey.”
Hold as she waits for Theo to answer, phone pressed to her ear. Fade out
without adding his reply or another reaction shot.

**Purpose:** Complete her choice of contact while leaving its outcome unresolved.

**Camera/blocking:** One shot, no internal cut. Fixed camera position,
upward tilt only. Rehearse camera height, distance and tilt range so both
the tabletop start and face ending are achievable from the same position.
Keep the phone/hand in view during the lift; do not treat a tilt as a zoom or
tracking move. Final framing: face and enough phone/hand to show listening.

**Sound/cut:** Acceptance stops the ringtone. “Hey,” then room tone and a
listening beat; no invented Theo dialogue. Fade out after the wait registers.

**Rough thumbnails — three stages of the SAME shot:**

```text
  A: accept/grasp       B: lift           C: say Hey / listen
+----------------+ +----------------+ +----------------+
| hand → [phone] | | hand + phone ↑ | |  face [phone]   |
| table         | |                | |        at ear  |
+----------------+ +----------------+ +----------------+
       └──────── CAMERA TILTS UP CONTINUOUSLY ────────┘
                NO CUTS BETWEEN A, B AND C
```

## Timing and readiness

Suggested first rehearsal ranges: 01 8–12 s; 02 2–3 s; 03 5–8 s;
04 3–5 s; 05 6–10 s. Total 24–38 s, a planning estimate only. Adjust to an
actual recorded ring cadence and performance; this list does not establish
a 2–3 minute finished film. If a longer runtime is required, develop and
approve the screenplay rather than padding these beats or inventing shots.
