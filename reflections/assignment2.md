# Assignment 2 reflection

### What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

My breakthrough was turning repeated corrections into durable instructions
and explicit acceptance criteria. I cleared Claude Code's context between
weeks and used Codex for cross-week tasks, but a recurring activity-format
requirement disappeared. That exposed a weakness in relying on conversation
history. I moved standing rules into `CLAUDE.md` and used short handoffs
for current decisions, next actions and verification limits. An outdated
manifest had also misled an agent, so incoming sessions must compare
records with actual files
([`38a8340`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-xuwancheng62-lab/commit/38a8340)).
The transferable lesson is to give the next session a reliable basis for
acting. The worksheet showed the same principle at task level: my specific
difficulty became an instruction whose result I could inspect.

### What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to take responsibility for intent, scope and acceptance throughout
agentic work. More reviews can consume attention without resolving the
important uncertainty. I limited routine content review to neighbouring
weeks and required reuse of relevant findings, accepting that distant
dependencies could be missed. Reliable handoff recovery and efficiency
gains remain unproven. My tests also protect the brief more strongly than
the course's distinctive teaching promises. Finally, I let completed work
accumulate into a large catch-up commit, weakening evidence of its sequence
([`38a8340`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-xuwancheng62-lab/commit/38a8340)).
In future projects, I want each completed task to end with verified
behaviour, a concise handoff and a scoped commit. These are habits I still
need to establish.
