# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

Work handed in after its due date is subject to a late penalty unless an
extension has been agreed in advance. If something genuinely gets in the way —
illness, an unexpected commitment, a technical failure outside your control —
contact the convenor before the deadline, not after it. Extensions are
considered case by case; the earlier you ask, the more options there are.

## Academic integrity

The direction is yours: the notes you give, the takes you select, and the
judgement calls behind both. Generated material is a legitimate part of every
week's process, not something to hide — but presenting someone else's
direction, or another student's selected takes, as your own is a breach of
academic integrity in the same way it would be for any other piece of
coursework. If you're ever unsure whether a particular use crosses a line,
ask before submitting.

## Getting help

Questions about a specific week's exercise are best asked during that week's
workshop, while the context is fresh. Anything that can't wait — a question
about the course itself, an assessment, or a personal circumstance — should go
to the convenor by email. See [People](/people/) for who's teaching and how to
reach them.
