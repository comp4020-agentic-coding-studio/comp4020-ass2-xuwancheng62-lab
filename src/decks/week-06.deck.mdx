# Planning before generating

Week 6 — SLOP4555

---

## Spend the hour, don't skip it

Every hour spent planning here is an hour not spent waiting on a queue for
a shot that was never going to be in the film.

This is the last week before generation starts. The discipline is to spend
it.

---

## The shot list is a production schedule

Not a wish list — order, dependency, what has to exist before what.

Redirecting a stick figure costs nothing. Redirecting a generated shot
costs a queue and a wait. Fix it on paper first.

---

## From rough thumbnails to a complete sequence

Week 4's storyboard covered one scene. This week expands it to the whole
short — continuity planning at the scale of a full sequence, not one shot
at a time.

---

## What Assignment 1 actually collects

| Submission | Locked in |
|---|---|
| Logline and premise | Week 2 |
| Screenplay | Week 3 |

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## The bar Assignment 1 sets

**Could a stranger pick up your bible and shot list and generate the same
characters and shots you intend, without asking you anything first?**

Nothing here is generated yet. It's judged as a production document.

---

## This week's task

Walk the class through your complete storyboard, shot by shot, without
stopping to explain anything the board doesn't already show. Where the
class gets lost, the board is missing information a generation will also be
missing.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## What you leave with

A shot list and storyboard that only needs fixing, not finishing, before
Assignment 1 is due.

Nothing new needs adding between now and then.
