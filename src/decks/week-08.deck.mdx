# Directing the AI performer

Week 8 — SLOP4555

---

## Every generation is a take, not a result

Rehearsal implies you expected some of them to fail. Treating each
generation as a surprise is the mistake this week corrects.

If nothing is ever rejected, nothing is actually being directed.

---

## Diagnose against objective and tactic

Mamet's frame: a shot argues something, through a tactic, toward an
objective. Use it as a rubric — does the take serve that argument?

| Area to investigate | What to check |
|---|---|
| The note | Playable circumstance, visible timing, conflicting instructions |
| The reference | Actual opening pose, geography, bible and supplied inputs |
| Model/workflow | Supported controls and variability under a clear setup |

Observation → possible cause with uncertainty → next check.
A mismatch alone does not prove which cause was responsible.

## A real rejected take

Week 7 B: real `sora-2` output, same medium starting frame.

- **Intent:** reach, stop short and hold without touching.
- **Observation:** around 3–4 seconds, fingers reach the phone edge and stay.
- **Decision:** reject; the stopping gap is missing.
- **Diagnosis:** motion failed to preserve the no-contact boundary; inspect
  note and reference before assigning a cause.
- **Later direction:** preserve a visible gap and hold hovering.

---

## Actionable direction, not "more/better"

"Make it better" has nowhere to land. A note aimed at a named failure —
*"hold the stillness half a second longer before the reach begins"* —
does.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

## Take selection is a discipline

Choosing the best of several takes and being able to say why — against the
beat's original intent, not against "which one looked best."

The same skill as a script supervisor's: not taste, accountability.

---

## This week's task

Bring one performance beat that hasn't worked cleanly yet. Generate three
takes. For each rejected one, record the mismatch, possible cause and next check.
In review: observe first, reveal direction/reference, then discuss causes. Select your take and justify it against the beat's objective and
tactic.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

## What you leave with

Three takes, a diagnosis for every rejection, and a selection you can
defend.

This diagnosis log is what a director's shot log looks like — keep the
habit for every beat from here on.
