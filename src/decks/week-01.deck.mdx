# What does a director actually do?

Week 1 — SLOP4555

---

## A director doesn't make the shot

A director says what a shot is for, decides whether it did that, and tells
someone exactly what to change.

- a cinematographer
- an actor
- a model

Same loop, same craft, regardless of who's on the other end.

## A note, not a wish

A note is precise enough to act on. A wish isn't.

| Wish | Note |
|---|---|
| "Make it more emotional" | "Look up half a second before you say it" |
| "It doesn't feel right" | "Hold the pause one beat longer before the line" |

A wish has nowhere to land. A note does.

---

## What changes with an AI cast and crew

- turnaround time — a reshoot costs minutes, not a call sheet
- the cost of trying something and being wrong

## What doesn't change

- judgement
- taste
- the discipline of saying precisely what's wrong

## "Table for One"

A daughter sits down for one last meal with the father she hasn't spoken to
in six years, on the night his diner closes for good.

Every week from here returns to it.

---

## This week's task

Generate one version of a small moment — a gesture, a line, an expression.
Write a director's note aimed at one specific thing. Generate again from the
note alone, not a rewritten description.

Compare the two takes. If nothing changed, the note wasn't specific enough.
