## Before the workshop

Bring the shots you've generated and directed so far for one sequence of your
film.

## In the workshop

Cut the shots together in order. Watch it back once, cold, and log every
continuity break you notice — a prop that moved, a light that shifted, a
line that doesn't match the mouth. For each one, decide fix or accept, and
write why.

## Afterwards

Only regenerate the shots your log actually flagged. The rest of the
sequence stays exactly as it is.
