## Before the workshop

Bring your near-final cut and your director's statement so far.

## In the workshop

Films screen back to back, no introductions. After each one, the class
names one decision they'd have made differently; the director defends the
choice actually made, against that specific alternative — not in general.
Write down the note that lands hardest, whether or not you end up acting on
it.

## Afterwards

The critique you gave and received today is itself evidence for the
reflection the final project asks for. You have until the final project
deadline to revise — spend it on the note that actually changes something,
not on polish nobody named.
