## Before the workshop

Bring a bible entry for every named character and location in your short.

## In the workshop

Swap bibles with a partner who knows nothing about your film. They describe
your protagonist back to you from the entry alone. Wherever their description
drifts from what you meant, the bible was vague, not their reading — fix the
entry, not the explanation you give afterward.

## Afterwards

These bibles are the reference every later generation gets handed back,
unchanged, every single time.
