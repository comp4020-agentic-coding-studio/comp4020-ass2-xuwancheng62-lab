## Before the workshop

Bring your approved shot list and bible from Assignment 1, and pick one shot
to work on today.

## In the workshop

Generate an anchor image for your chosen shot straight from your bible
entries. Animate it. Then write one performance note — a timing change, a
held look, a delayed gesture — and generate a second take from that note
alone.

## Afterwards

You leave with an anchor image and two takes that differ by exactly one
directed choice — the same discipline from week 1, now with motion attached.
