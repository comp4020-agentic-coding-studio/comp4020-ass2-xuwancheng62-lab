## Before the workshop

Bring the scene and intent note from week 3.

## In the workshop

Break the scene into shots on paper — number, type, purpose, one line each —
then sketch a rough thumbnail underneath each entry: a frame, a stick figure
or two, an arrow if a body or the camera moves. The thumbnail doesn't need to
be good, only legible enough that someone else could stage the shot from it.
Read your list to a partner without showing them the scene; if they can't
tell what each shot is doing from the number, purpose and thumbnail
together, merge or cut it. A shot with no stated purpose is coverage, not
direction.

## Afterwards

This list and its thumbnails are a first-draft storyboard. Week 6 expands it
into a complete sequence for the whole short, and Assignment 1 collects that
expanded version — keep this one, don't start over.
