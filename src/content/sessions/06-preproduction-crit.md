## Before the workshop

Bring your complete shot list and storyboard — the whole short, not a scene
of it.

## In the workshop

Walk the class through your storyboard in order, shot by shot, without
stopping to explain anything the board doesn't already show. Where the class
gets lost, the board is missing information a generation will also be
missing.

## Afterwards

This is the package Assignment 1 submits. It's due when teaching resumes
after the mid-semester break, not this week — but nothing new needs to be
added between now and then, only fixed.
