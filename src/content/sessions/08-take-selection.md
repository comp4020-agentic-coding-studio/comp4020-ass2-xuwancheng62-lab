## Before the workshop

Bring one performance beat from your film that hasn't worked cleanly yet.

## In the workshop

Generate three takes of the beat. For each rejected one, write a one-line
diagnosis — wrong emotion, bad timing, a continuity slip, the model ignoring
the note. Select your take and write the justification against the original
intent, not against the other takes.

## Afterwards

The diagnosis log you wrote today is what a director's shot log looks like —
keep the habit for every beat from here on.
