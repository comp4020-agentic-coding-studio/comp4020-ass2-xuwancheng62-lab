## Before the workshop

Bring the scene from your own short that carries the most weight — usually
the one where the want and the obstacle actually meet.

## In the workshop

Write the scene in class, in proper form. Once it's down, add one intent
note in the margin next to a line or action that matters — not what happens,
but how it should land and why. Trade pages with a classmate and see whether
your note is specific enough that they'd direct the same performance from it.

## Afterwards

The scene and its intent note travel forward together: next week you break
this exact scene into shots.
