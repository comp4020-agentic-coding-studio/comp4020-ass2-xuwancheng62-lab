## Before the workshop

Bring the continuity-checked sequence from week 9.

## In the workshop

Cut it twice. First cut: hold every reaction a beat longer than feels
natural. Second cut: cut away before the reaction lands. Screen both back to
back and write one sentence per cut on what it does to the audience's read of
the scene.

## Afterwards

Pick whichever cut actually serves your film's intent — not whichever felt
more impressive to make.
