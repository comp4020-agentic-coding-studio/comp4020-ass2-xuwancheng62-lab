## Before the workshop

Bring your chosen cut from week 10, with one line still undirected.

## In the workshop

Before mixing anything, direct the voice: generate two takes of that line
from a single note — pace, breath, or a hesitation before the line lands —
the same discipline as week 1, now aimed at a voice instead of a face.
Select the take that serves the beat and lay it in. Then add one ambience
bed, one Foley or SFX moment, and one music cue placed on purpose, not under
the whole scene by default. Watch the dialogue-only and full-pass versions
back to back.

## Afterwards

Wherever the sound pass changed the meaning of a beat, note it — that note is
part of what the final director's statement asks for.
