## The brief

> Direct the film on paper before you direct it in generation.

Weeks 2 through 6 are all pre-production, and this is where that work lands.
Generative tools have no memory between calls — a model doesn't know your
lead character had a scar in the last shot unless you tell it again, in the
same words, every time. The discipline this assignment tests is whether you
can plan tightly enough that continuity survives that amnesia, before it
costs you a week of wasted generations in the second half of the course.

## What you submit

- your locked **logline and premise**
- the **screenplay** for your short (scenes in standard format, with
  director's intent notes)
- a **character and world bible**: enough concrete, repeatable detail about
  each character, location, costume and prop that two separate generations
  would produce the same thing
- a full **shot list and storyboard** for the entire short, sequenced and
  ready to generate from

Nothing here is generated yet. This assignment is judged as a production
document — could someone else pick it up and shoot your film from it?
