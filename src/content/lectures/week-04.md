A scene on the page is one thing happening. A scene on screen is a sequence
of decisions about what the audience is allowed to see, and when. This week
turns last week's page into that sequence.

## Outline

- the shot vocabulary — wide, medium, close-up, insert — and what each one is
  actually *for*, not just variety for its own sake
- composition and angle as argument, not decoration: where the frame's edge
  falls says something about power, distance, intimacy
- blocking and camera movement as one decision, not two — where a body moves
  and where the camera moves have to agree
- storyboarding a shot as a quick thumbnail — a frame, a rough figure, an
  arrow for movement — legible enough for someone else to stage it from
- breaking the plate-slide from "Table for One" into a shot sequence: which
  shot carries the emotional weight, and which ones just get you there
