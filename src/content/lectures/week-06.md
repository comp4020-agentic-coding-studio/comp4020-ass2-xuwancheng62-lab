Every hour spent planning here is an hour not spent waiting on a queue for a
shot that was never going to be in the film. This week is the last one before
generation starts, and the discipline is to spend it, not skip it.

## Outline

- the shot list as a production schedule, not a wish list: order, dependency,
  what has to exist before what
- expanding the rough storyboard you started in week 4 into a complete
  sequence — redirecting a stick figure costs nothing; redirecting a
  generated shot costs a queue and a wait
- continuity planning at the scale of a whole sequence, not one shot at a
  time
- this week's work is exactly what Assignment 1 collects: logline,
  screenplay, bible, shot list, storyboard — due when teaching resumes after
  the mid-semester break, not this week
