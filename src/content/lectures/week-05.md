A human crew remembers what a costume looked like yesterday. A model doesn't
carry anything between calls unless you hand it back every time, in the same
words. That single fact is why this week exists before a single frame gets
generated.

## Outline

- a character bible entry as a set of facts specific enough that two separate
  generations produce the same person: age, build, wardrobe, one
  distinguishing feature, voice
- a world bible doing the same job for a location: what's on the walls, what
  the light looks like, what has to stay fixed shot to shot
- why this is the hard problem specifically for an AI cast and crew, and not
  merely good practice: there is no continuity unless you re-supply it
- "Table for One": bibles for Del, Mara and the diner — what has to hold
  steady for the closing scene to read as one place, not three
