Shots generated separately need to look like they happened in the same room,
on the same night. This week is about noticing exactly where they don't, and
fixing only that — not regenerating a sequence that's already mostly right.

## Outline

- assembling separately generated shots into one sequence: what has to match
  (wardrobe, light, geography) and what the eye simply forgives
- recognising character drift and spatial or temporal continuity failures,
  rather than reacting to a vague "it looks a bit off"
- transitions as a directorial choice, not whatever the software defaults to
- the repair discipline: fix the shot that's actually broken, not the four
  around it that already work
