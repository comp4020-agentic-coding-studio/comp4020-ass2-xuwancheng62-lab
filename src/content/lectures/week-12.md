The last week turns the critique skill from week 9 back onto your own film,
and asks the question this whole course has been building toward: which
choices in the finished work are yours, and which are limitations you
learned to work around. Screening happens today; the final cut is due
eleven days later, and that gap is the revision period, not slack — the
critique you get today is what it's for.

## Outline

- a last continuity and quality pass across the whole film, checked against
  the bible locked in Assignment 1
- critique as a directing skill turned on your own work: naming a decision,
  not just a feeling about it
- the revision period: picking the one note from today's critique worth
  acting on, and leaving the rest
- authorship: separating a directorial choice from a tool limitation you
  routed around
- what actually transfers: the direct → generate → evaluate → redirect loop
  doesn't belong to this semester's specific tools
