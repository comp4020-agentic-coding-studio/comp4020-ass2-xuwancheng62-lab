The same shots, cut two different ways, tell two different stories about
what a character feels. Nothing gets regenerated this week — the footage from
week 9 is the raw material, and the decisions are all in the arrangement.

## Outline

- a cut is a decision about what the audience notices next, not just where
  one shot happens to end
- pacing and shot duration as emotional instruments: holding on a reaction
  versus cutting away from it early
- montage and rhythm: what repetition and juxtaposition do that a single long
  take can't
- recutting the diner sequence two ways: cutting to Mara's reaction
  immediately, versus holding on Del first
