## BIS-W02-LOGLINE

Canonical text: [logline.txt](./logline.txt).

On the final night before her late mother's apartment is cleared, Nadia wants to answer her estranged brother's call but must overcome her own restraint after years of silence, or let this chance for contact pass when the ringing stops.

### Initial draft (superseded; retained for teaching comparison)

On the final night before her late mother's apartment is cleared, a woman must decide whether to answer a call from the brother she hasn't spoken to in years, before the ringing stops.

The draft's "must decide whether" leaves the want ambiguous. The current
lecture and deck demonstrate the revision; the downloadable logline is
the revised reference, not this draft.

## BIS-W02-PREMISE

Canonical text: [premise.txt](./premise.txt).

On the final night before her late mother's apartment is cleared, Nadia finishes packing the kitchen and sits alone at its table when her estranged brother Theo calls. She wants to answer, but holds herself back from speaking to someone she has avoided for years. As the phone continues to ring, reaching for it and withdrawing make the conflict visible: she can keep their silence intact, or risk making contact while the call is still there to answer. On the last ring before the call would go to voicemail, she finally accepts and says "Hey", then listens. The moment ends with her choosing contact, before either sibling has to resolve their years of estrangement.

## Scope and teaching checks

- Protagonist: Nadia; immediate want: answer Theo's call.
- Obstacle: her own restraint after years of estrangement. The ringing
  supplies a deadline, not a second antagonist.
- Stakes: this immediate opportunity for contact may pass. This is not a
  claim that she could never call back.
- Filmability: target 2–4 minutes, one kitchen, one performer on screen;
  Theo is the off-screen caller. No flashbacks, second location, or
  reconciliation scene is required. Runtime has not been production-tested.
- The revised logline names the want, self-restraint, and immediate
  stakes explicitly; it no longer needs the premise to supply the want.
- The premise explicitly carries the already-established canonical ending:
  Nadia accepts the call and says "Hey", as in the Week 3 scene. It does
  not resolve the relationship. The Week 1 workshop remains a separate,
  counterfactual voicemail-callback exercise.
- Lecture and deck model a 2:10 scope estimate: 0:00–1:25 final kitchen
  packing; 1:25–1:55 the ringing and hesitation; 1:55–2:10 accepting,
  speaking, and listening. These are planning durations, not a recorded
  enactment or measured production. The opening must earn its time in
  testing, rather than padding the ringing to reach the runtime.
- Students bring their own logline and premise, as session 02 requires.
