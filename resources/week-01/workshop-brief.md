Current instructor brief, superseding the earlier Take A/three-candidate

## Current requirements

Use the existing `workshop-take-a.mp4` and served
`public/resources/week-01/workshop-take-a-web.mp4` unchanged. No replacement
Take A, Take B generation, candidate selection, or A/B video comparison
is required. Existing anchor, prompt, settings and original exports are
retained as production records, not required student downloads.

The scene remains an explicitly counterfactual voicemail-callback variation.
Students first watch cold, then receive the fixed objective: Nadia wants
to call her brother back once she can speak without her voice breaking.
The intended reading is steadying herself to reconnect, rather than avoidance.

Students identify one observable performance problem in the actual clip,
write a playable redirect, receive a partner's check, revise the note,
and describe what they would look for in a future take. Do not require
one predetermined diagnosis or present predicted effects as verified results.
The student-facing instructions and four completion criteria are in
`src/content/sessions/01-getting-started.md` (120 minutes).
