# Week 4 teaching asset - shot list and storyboard template

Version: 1.0, added 2026-09-12. This is a blank scaffolding template for
students' own shot lists; it is not evidence of the owner's own shot list
and does not satisfy `OWN-W04`.

## BIS-W04-TEMPLATE

Canonical file: [shot-list-template.html](./shot-list-template.html).

A downloadable, print-friendly HTML page for turning a scene into a shot
list and rough storyboard. Six repeated shot blocks, each with fields for
shot number, shot type, visual content, one-clause purpose, character/
camera movement, and a generous bordered sketch box, plus a footer
reminder that no two shots on a list should share a purpose (the same
non-repetition rule the lecture and `04-shot-breakdown.md`'s `spec:`
bullets both check). Plain black-on-white serif layout with a `@media
print` block; no dependency on the site's brand fonts or theme, so it
prints cleanly on its own.

## Scope and teaching checks

- Matches the two `spec:` bullets in `04-shot-breakdown.md`'s frontmatter
  exactly: a numbered shot list with type and rough thumbnail per shot, and
  one-clause purpose per shot with none repeated.
- Scaffolding only, not a graded artefact and not owner evidence: it does
  not satisfy `OWN-W04` (the owner's own numbered shot list and thumbnails,
  built on their own `OWN-W03` scene). It is a teaching aid for reaching
  the outcomes `04-shot-breakdown.md` already checks, matching how
  `BIS-W03-WORKSHEET` (`resources/week-03/materials.md`) relates to
  `OWN-W03`.
- Six blocks is a starting allowance, not a hard cap - the "Before It
  Stops" demonstration alone runs to five shots, so a scene with more
  beats needs more copies of the block (print or photocopy additional
  pages).
